Overview

Overview

CHARM — the Central Hub for American Research on Men — is a new research center co-founded by Antenna Up and Gallup. It is being built to become the country’s definitive, independent source of evidence on the health of American men and boys, and the platform that carries that evidence into the national conversation.

The pages that follow move in a simple order: why this matters now, the gap that holds the field back, what CHARM is and how it works, the people behind it, and the value it creates. A closing section explains how a small group of partners can help establish it. Scope, structure and figures are illustrative and shaped together.

The Need

American men are losing ground — and it touches everyone

By nearly every measure, American men and boys are falling behind. They trail at every stage of education, earn a declining share of college degrees, and participate in the workforce at rates far below those of prior generations. They die younger, account for the large majority of deaths of despair, and report a steep decline in friendship and community. None of this stands against anyone else’s progress — but it carries direct consequences for family formation, workforce strength and the health of civil society. The wellbeing of men is inseparable from the wellbeing of women, families and communities.

The issue has become a genuine national concern — the subject of best-selling books, new institutes and rising attention from employers and leaders. The moment is here. What is missing is the foundation beneath it.

The Problem

A consequential conversation, running ahead of the evidence

For all the attention, the field has no shared, credible fact base. The research that exists is scattered — spread across government tables, academic journals and one-off studies, measured in inconsistent ways, and rarely examined by gender. Organizations build programs, funders make commitments and leaders speak to the issue without agreed-upon numbers to work from.

The effect is twofold. The national conversation runs well ahead of what is actually known — and into that gap step the loudest voices rather than the most reliable ones. And because there is no common evidence base, the field stays fragmented and underfunded, unable to point to a single trusted source or a clear measure of whether things are getting better or worse.

The bottleneck is not attention. It is evidence — rigorous, independent, and in one place.

The Solution

CHARM: original research and a permanent platform for the state of men in America

CHARM closes the gap with two things the field has never had together: a program of rigorous, original national research on American men, and a standing communications platform that turns those findings into a national conversation — year after year.

At its foundation is new, independent measurement conducted by Gallup — and an annual American Men’s Health Index that becomes the number the field, the press and policymakers measure against over time. Around that foundation sits a permanent platform: a program of reports, a calendar of convenings, and the media reach to carry each finding to the people positioned to act on it. Together they make CHARM the authoritative source of record on the state of American men.

The Research

Original national measurement, built to be trusted

CHARM’s foundation is a new, recurring program of original research on American men — conducted by Gallup, on the largest probability-based research panel in the United States, and designed from the outset to withstand scrutiny.

This is a standing measurement program, not a single survey. Each year, Gallup conducts new national research on a large, representative sample of American men — with additional depth among young men, the group at the center of the concern — producing original data no existing source captures. Because the American Men’s Health Index must be beyond dispute, its inputs are drawn entirely from this Gallup-conducted research, so there is one clear, documented methodology behind every number. And because Gallup has measured how Americans live, work and worship since 1935, every new finding lands against decades of trend rather than starting from zero.

The Index

The American Men’s Health Index

A single, authoritative annual measure of the state of American men — across physical, mental and spiritual health, with spiritual understood as purpose and belonging.

The Index is CHARM’s signature: one credible number, built entirely on Gallup’s original research, that captures how American men are doing and moves year over year. It gives the country something it has never had — a clear, trusted answer to whether the state of American men is improving or declining, and a benchmark that can hold governments, employers and communities to account. Released annually and fully documented, it is designed to become the figure the press, employers and policymakers cite as a matter of course.

The Platform Partner

Antenna Up and Janna Meyrowitz Turner

Rigorous research changes little on its own. Antenna Up supplies the other half of the equation — the national community, the media relationships and the public voice that carry the findings into the culture.

Antenna Up is a cultural platform dedicated to the health and wellbeing of American men and boys. In a short time it has convened a national community of leaders, employers and advocates around the issue, and demonstrated that serious institutions will engage it when the platform is credible and the tone is constructive. It brings CHARM a ready audience, a program of national convenings, and standing relationships across the media that shape the national conversation.

At its head is Janna Meyrowitz Turner, CHARM’s public spokesperson. A communications strategist with two decades of experience building coalitions and shaping public narratives, she previously founded and led a nationally recognized agency whose counsel spanned the worlds of consumer brands, culture and sport. She carries the Center’s findings with a forward-looking, solutions-oriented voice — one built on opportunity, resilience and results — paired with, and amplifying, Gallup’s institutional standing.

Why This Pairing

The definitive evidence, and a platform equal to carrying it.

Rigorous research and cultural reach rarely sit under one roof. Independent measurement of this caliber has, until now, had no dedicated platform to carry it into the national conversation; the platforms that command that conversation have lacked measurement of this authority to stand on. CHARM was created to hold both at once — evidence that can be cited by all sides and disputed by none, and the standing to ensure it is heard. It is an institution built to endure, and to define its subject for a generation.

The condition of American men is now a defining question of our national life. CHARM exists to ensure the answer is measured with authority, held to the highest standard, and heard where it matters — and to invite a select few to help establish it.
